# Trader Clock

A customizable trading time zone clock that aligns market sessions to your local timezone.  
Features include:
- 📍 Market session bands (New York, London, Tokyo, Sydney, etc.)
- 🔴 Red dots for scheduled high-impact news events
- 📊 Hourly volume bars
- 💹 Bids vs sales overlays
- ⚙️ Customizable sessions and strategy filters

## Getting Started
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser.
3. Adjust settings in the left panel to match your timezone and strategy.
